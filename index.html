About us

A Ghanaian food company with a sunny outlook

Erdich Foods is based at Amarhia, off the Adenta–Dodowa Road. We make honest, nourishing food for families, individuals, corporate institutions, schools and events. It's food you can trust and enjoy every day.

Our first brand, D-cleo Yogurt, is a creamy probiotic drinking yogurt. It comes plain or flavoured (Apple, Strawberry, Mango and Vanilla), sweetened or unsweetened, and with or without wheat berries. More products from the Erdich NourishNest are on the way.
